The methodology

Serious athletes want to know how they'll train. This page shows the rigor behind it.

Data-driven approach

What actually gets analyzed

WKO5 and TrainingPeaks turn raw session files into decisions — not just charts.

Power-duration curve

Your true capabilities at every duration, tracked over time — not a single guessed FTP number.

Lactate & metabolic zones

Zones set from your own physiological thresholds, re-tested as fitness changes — not generic percentages of max HR.

HRV & fatigue trends

Daily readiness data feeds directly into whether a session gets held, adjusted or skipped.

The process

Three phases of training

  1. 1

    Initial evaluation

    A field or lab test, plus a full review of your historical training data — the real starting point, not a self-reported estimate.

  2. 2

    Dynamic periodization

    The plan adjusts to your physiological response, accumulated fatigue and real life — reviewed weekly, not set once and left alone.

  3. 3

    Race-day tactics

    Pacing, power/pace targets and nutrition strategy, built specifically for your goal race.
